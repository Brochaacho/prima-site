How do I unlock the full story?

Ballet Class and The Nutcracker are free. Tap any locked ballet on the map, choose Ask a grown-up, answer the grown-up question, and complete the one-time App Store purchase.

I bought it on another device

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase, or go to Settings and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Moving progress to a new device

Open More → Saves, copy the backup code, then paste it into the same screen on the new device.

No sound?

Check the volume, the silent switch, and the Music and Sounds sliders in Settings.

Too hard?

Turn on Easy mode in Settings for extra help and a soft landing after falls.
