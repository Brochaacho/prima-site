Privacy Policy

Prima — a ballet adventure · Last updated November 4, 2026

The short version: Prima does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts and no chat.

What stays on your device

Game progress (stars, badges, costumes, settings and the names you type for player profiles) is saved only on your device so you can keep playing. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Backup codes

The game can show a backup code that contains your progress. It is created on your device and only goes where you choose to copy or paste it.

Purchases

The optional "full story" unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check so children cannot buy by accident.

Children

Prima is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Third parties

Prima contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software and does not link to other websites or apps.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? See the support page.
